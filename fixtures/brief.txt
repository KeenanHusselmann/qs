MONAGHAN FARM — AFFORDABLE HOUSING SCHEME
Developer Brief and Concept Parameters

1. SCHEME INTENT
Clever Development Trust proposes an affordable housing scheme on Erf 180,
Monaghan Extension 3. The scheme comprises 1 046 residential units delivered
across three phases, targeting the GAP market with household incomes between
R8 000 and R22 000 per month.

2. UNIT COUNTS AND MIX
Phase 1 delivers 412 units, Phase 2 delivers 340 units and Phase 3 delivers
294 units. The accommodation schedule provides for 2-bedroom walk-up units of
48 m2 and 3-bedroom units of 62 m2 in a ratio of four to one.

3. SITE LOCATION
The site extends to 63.8 hectares with a developable area of 41.2 hectares
after servitudes and the wetland buffer are excluded.

4. MARKET STUDY AND ABSORPTION
The market study prepared by the developer indicates an absorption rate of
between 71 and 136 units per annum in the surrounding catchment. Comparable
sales in adjacent schemes achieved between R540 000 and R680 000 per unit.
