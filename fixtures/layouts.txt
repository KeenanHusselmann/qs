MONAGHAN FARM — AFFORDABLE HOUSING SCHEME
Typology schedule and concept layouts

1. TYPOLOGY SCHEDULE
Type A: 2-bedroom walk-up, gross floor area 48 m2.
Type B: 3-bedroom walk-up, gross floor area 62 m2.
Type A and Type B are provided in a ratio of four to one across all three
phases, totalling 1 046 residential units.

2. BLOCK ARRANGEMENT
Units are arranged in three-storey walk-up blocks of twelve units each,
with no lifts and no basement parking. Parking is on grade at 0.8 bays
per unit.

3. SPECIFICATION LEVEL
Finishes are to the GAP-market standard assumed in the cost plan: ceramic
floor tiles to wet areas, screeded and sealed floors elsewhere, prepaid
electricity metering and solar water heating to each unit.

4. DENSITY AND LAND USE
The land use rights permit a density of 55 units per hectare over the
developable area of 41.2 hectares. Permitted use is Residential 3.
