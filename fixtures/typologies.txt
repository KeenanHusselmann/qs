ANYTOWN EXTENSION 1 — AFFORDABLE HOUSING
Typology schedule and concept layouts

1. TYPOLOGY SCHEDULE
Type A: 2-bedroom walk-up, gross floor area 47 m2, 620 units.
Type B: 3-bedroom walk-up, gross floor area 61 m2, 180 units.
Type C: 1-bedroom simplex, gross floor area 38 m2, 96 units.
The accommodation schedule totals 896 residential units across the three types.

2. DENSITY AND LAND USE
The land use rights permit a density of 55 units per hectare over the
developable area. Permitted use is Residential 3.

3. PROGRAMME
The construction programme runs to 34 months from site handover, delivered in
three phases of roughly equal duration.

4. BULK SERVICES
Development charges are assessed at R38 500 per unit, payable on approval of
the general plan.
