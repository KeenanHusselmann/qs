AGREEMENT AND SCHEDULE — MONAGHAN FARM PHASE 1
JBCC Principal Building Agreement, Edition 6.2, May 2018

1. THE PARTIES
The Employer is Clever Development Trust. The Contractor is Ndlovu Construction
(Pty) Ltd, registration 2009/114872/07, of Midrand, Gauteng.

2. CONTRACT SUM
The contract sum is R186 420 000,00 excluding VAT, being the accepted tender
amount without adjustment.

3. SECURITY AND RETENTION
Retention is 10% of the value of work executed, reducing to 5% at practical
completion. The limit of retention is R9 321 000,00, being 5% of the contract
sum, and retention shall not exceed it at any time.

4. DATES
The construction period is 30 calendar months. The date for practical
completion is 12 March 2029, calculated from a commencement date of
12 September 2026.

5. PENALTY
The penalty for late completion is R42 000,00 per calendar day.

6. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
CPAP applies. The base date for contract price adjustment is 1 August 2026.

7. PAYMENT
Payment is due within 21 days of the date of the certificate. Interest on
overdue amounts accrues at prime plus 2%.

8. ADVANCE PAYMENT
An advance payment of R9 000 000,00 is payable on establishment, recovered
pro rata against the first six certificates.

9. SIGNATURE
Signed at Midrand on 28 August 2026 by the duly authorised representatives of
both parties.
