VARIATION ORDER No. VO-009 — MONAGHAN FARM PHASE 1

INSTRUCTION
This variation is issued under contract instruction CI-014 dated
3 February 2027.

DESCRIPTION OF VARIATION
The varied work is the substitution of 8mm ceramic floor tiling with 10mm
porcelain tiling to all Type A bathrooms, including making good to abutting
finishes.

REASON
Client instruction following the show unit review. Not a design development
item and not a latent condition.

VALUE
Add R1 924 600,00. Omit R412 000,00 in respect of the superseded ceramic
tiling. The net value of this variation is an addition of R1 512 600,00.

TIME
No extension of time is claimed.

STATUS
Approved by the principal agent on 19 February 2027.
