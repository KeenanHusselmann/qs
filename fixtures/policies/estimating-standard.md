# Mzilikazi Property Services — Estimating Standard

## 1. Scope

This standard governs cost estimating on all feasibility and pre-tender work undertaken by the
practice. It applies to every project regardless of client, sector or value.

## 2. Estimate classes

All pre-feasibility estimates are prepared to ASAQS Class 4 or Class 5. A Class 4 estimate carries
an accuracy band of minus fifteen to plus twenty-five per cent and must be described as indicative
wherever it is reported. No pre-feasibility estimate may be described as a tender price.

## 3. Contingency

Every estimate carries a contingency. Where the priced cost plan already includes a contingency
line, no further contingency is added — double-counting a contingency overstates the cost and is
treated as an error, not a conservatism.

## 4. Sign-off

No estimate leaves the practice unsigned. A named quantity surveyor signs each step, and their
signature freezes the figures that step produced. A figure that has not been signed is not approved
data and must not be reported to a client.

## 5. Programme and absorption

A viability opinion requires a programme and an absorption rate. Where either is absent the opinion
is withheld: a scheme cannot be described as viable without knowing when its money moves.

## 6. Bulk services

Bulk services contributions are reported separately from construction cost and are never netted off
against it. Where a municipality is providing any part of the bulk services, that must be stated.
