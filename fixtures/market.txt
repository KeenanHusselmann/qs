RESIDENTIAL MARKET STUDY — MONAGHAN FARM CATCHMENT
Prepared for Clever Development Trust.

1. ABSORPTION
Comparable schemes in the catchment achieved an absorption rate of between
71 and 136 units per annum, with the upper figure recorded only where a show
unit was open before first transfer.

2. COMPARABLE SALES
Comparable sales in adjacent schemes achieved between R540 000 and R680 000
per unit over the preceding eighteen months. The achievable selling price for
a 48 m2 two-bedroom walk-up is assessed at R620 000 per unit.

3. DEMAND
Household income in the primary catchment falls between R8 000 and R22 000 per
month, which is the GAP band the scheme targets.
